Check before you tick

This container couldn't read which USB device your server boots from, so it can't leave it out of the list below. Every device here is shown as found, and one of them may be your Unraid boot flash.

Passing the boot flash through to a VM takes the server down, so don't tick a device unless you know what it is. Anything you recognise as your own dongle is safe to pass through as normal.
